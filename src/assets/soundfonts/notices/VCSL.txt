Source revision: c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e
Samples dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. See CC0.txt.

Included: Cajón and clap examples; recorded congas, bongos, darbuka, frame drum,
guiro, claves, agogo, cowbell, shaker, tambourine, and gong. The source table
and SHA-256 values are pinned in scripts/lib/percussion-sources.json.
MixGenres modifications: mono 44.1 kHz conversion, trailing-silence trimming,
5 ms terminal fades, velocity zones and deterministic round-robin take selection.
